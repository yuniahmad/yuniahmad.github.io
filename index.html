What I’m working on

Research

That curiosity also shapes my PhD in Art and Design at UiTM. I study how art and design students use text-to-image AI during creative ideation and visual exploration. My goal is to develop practical guidelines for using these tools responsibly in art and design education.

PhD in Art and Design · UiTM · Thesis in progress

Exploring the Role of Generative AI Text-to-Image Technology in Enhancing Creativity Among Art and Design Students in Higher Education

This qualitative research explores how text-to-image tools shape creative ideation and visual exploration among art and design students. It examines students’ engagement and perceptions and aims to develop guidelines for responsible AI integration in art and design education.

Additional focus

AI and digital heritage

A socio-technical framework using generative AI and NFTs to support Malaysian batik artisans, digital ownership, provenance and cultural preservation.
